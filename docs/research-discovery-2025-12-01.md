# Research Discovery: Duolingo Clone (SEA Focus)

## Product Overview
**Product Name:** Duolingo Clone (SEA Focus)
**Description:** A gamified language learning application modeled after Duolingo, specifically tailored for the Southeast Asian market. The platform aims to leverage the proven gamification model while adapting content, pricing, and user experience to the specific needs and cultural nuances of SEA learners.

## Research Objectives
1.  **Market Sizing:** Quantify the addressable market for language learning apps in Southeast Asia, specifically focusing on the demand for English (ESL) and other high-value languages.
2.  **Competitive Landscape:** Identify and analyze key competitors in the region, including global giants (Duolingo, Babbel) and strong local/regional players (e.g., ELSA Speak, Topica).
3.  **User Insights:** Understand the specific motivations ("Jobs to be Done") of SEA learners—is it primarily for career advancement, education, or travel?
4.  **Localization & Pricing:** Determine the optimal business model (freemium vs. paid) and pricing sensitivity for the region, as well as technical constraints (device types, connectivity).

## Research Scope
*   **Primary Market:** Southeast Asia (SEA).
*   **Key Countries:** Indonesia, Vietnam, Thailand, Philippines, Malaysia, Singapore.
*   **Target Audience:** Mobile-first users, students, and young professionals looking to upskill.
*   **Key Domain:** Mobile Language Learning (EdTech).
